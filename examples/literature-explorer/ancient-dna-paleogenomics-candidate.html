Ancient DNA and paleogenomics

Bibliography index · proposed route /origins/biblio/ancient_dna_paleogenomics.html

Ancient DNA and paleogenomics covers the recovery, authentication, and analysis of DNA from ancient biological material, with emphasis on extinct hominin genomes, Neanderthal and Denisovan ancestry, ancient population history, and the methods used to distinguish endogenous ancient molecules from contamination and damage. The topic complements the Archive’s existing human evolution, DNA, genetics, and paleoanthropology bibliographies by gathering direct genomic evidence that became central after many classic TalkOrigins pages were written.
